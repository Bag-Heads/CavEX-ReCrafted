# Front-end landscape

menu-background.png is an in-game render of CaveWii's own world generator, not painted artwork. Scene: Overworld, seed 42, player eye position (0.5, 68.62, 64.5), engine yaw 270 degrees, polar pitch 94 degrees (4 degrees down), world time 6000, clear weather, HUD and hand hidden, rendered with the bundled Earth & Ash textures. Capture: normal 640x480 PC viewport, crop (0,96)-(640,456) to 640x360, then nearest-neighbour upscale to 1024x576 RGB PNG. No terrain pixels were painted over or retouched. Recipe and scripts: tmp/jobs/design-round-1027/astra (NOTES.md, prepare.py, capture.ps1, compose.py). Source file: tmp/jobs/design-round-1027/astra/menu-background-earth-ash.png.

Licence: Earth & Ash textures are CC0 1.0, and the scene is generated by CaveWii code, so this derivative is dedicated under CC0 1.0. No Mojang pixels were used.

This is the bundled Earth & Ash fallback. A user pack may supply its own gui/menu-background.png through the ordinary pack-first lookup (packs/isabella-ii ships a matching render of the same scene with Isabella II textures). Menus hold the image still; in-game menus retain the actual world.

Machine-readable source and output identity: menu-background.provenance.json.
